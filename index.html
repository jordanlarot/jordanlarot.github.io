Jordan Larot

Director of International Business @ Maniformer (AgiBot) · San Francisco, CA

I build commercial partnerships at the intersection of robotics data and physical AI, connecting frontier AI labs, research institutions, and enterprises with the infrastructure and hardware they need to train real-world embodied systems.

Current Role

I'm Director of International Business at Maniformer (a subsidiary of AgiBot), building out the company's U.S. commercial presence around embodied AI data infrastructure – egocentric video, UMI datasets, and teleoperation data – as well as AgiBot's G-series hardware. I serve as the technical-business bridge between external partners and our engineering teams, translating what partners need into what the data and hardware can deliver.

Background

Previously, I worked at Open Droids as a Research Engineer, building teleoperation data pipelines and training manipulation policies for a bimanual robot (R2D3) – handling evals, debugging, and deployment along the way. This included building an agentic intelligence system, training policies (ACT, π0.5, and Isaac GR00T 1.7), and building real-time monitoring dashboards.

Before that, I was a Robotics Engineer at BitRobot Foundation, working on real-world robot systems in the Hugging Face LeRobot ecosystem – building and maintaining mobile robots (LeKiwi, XLeRobot), assembling and testing SO-101 arms, and supporting data collection, debugging, and system reliability.
